# INC-019 — Expired internal service certificate

## Summary
['Restarts loaded the same expired certificate.', 'Timeout changes were unrelated to TLS verification.']

## Root Cause
An internal certificate renewal job failed silently.

## Successful Resolution
- Confirmed certificate expiry
- Renewed the certificate
- Added renewal-job failure alerting

## Failed Attempts
- Restarted inventory pods
- Changed service timeout
- Disabled retries
